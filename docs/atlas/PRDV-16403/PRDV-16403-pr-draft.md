## House style, learned the hard way

The first pass at these bodies was **rejected as overbuilt** and rewritten on 2026-08-24. Validated against real merged PRs in both repos (`atlas-front-end` #560/#555/#554, `callisto-back-end` #429/#427/#425), the expected shape is:

- **Clickup** - the bare ClickUp URL.
- **Description** - the job story ("As an X, I want Y, so that Z") plus a short paragraph or two on what changed. Prose, not headings.
- **Test Evidence** / **Testing and Verification** - **screenshots are the norm**, or short bullets of what was checked.
- **Checklist** - the template boxes.

**Do not put in a PR body:** commit hashes, verification-gate tables, exact gate commands, Problem/Requirement/Solution headings, or a catalogue of caveats. Those belong in the changelog. A reviewer wants what changed and how it was checked.
